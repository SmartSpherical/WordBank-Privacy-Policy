WordBank Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

WordBank is a vocabulary app published by SmartSphere. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your words stay on your phone. WordBank has no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data. Some features contact other services, and the free version shows ads from Google, as explained below.

Information stored on your phone

Your words, sentences, definitions, groups, practice progress and settings are stored on your device. We can't see them. If you add your own Google Cloud translation key, it's stored in encrypted form on your device.

If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include this information in its backup to your Google account. You control this in your phone's settings.

Information sent to other services

  • Looking up words. To find definitions and translations, the word is sent to Wiktionary, the Free Dictionary API (freedictionaryapi.com or dictionaryapi.dev) or Jisho. For words that aren't in English, the word is also sent to MyMemory, a translation service, to work out which meaning is the most common. If you add your own Google Cloud translation key, this goes to Google under your own account instead. Only the word is sent, not who you are, though these services can see your IP address, as any website can.
  • Stroke order. For calligraphy practice, the character being traced is requested from jsDelivr.
  • Translating sentences. When you view a sentence in another language, the sentence is sent to MyMemory, a translation service, unless it's a built-in phrase or you've edited the translation yourself. Please don't save anything private as a sentence. If you add your own Google Cloud translation key, sentences go to Google under your own account instead.
  • Translating on your device. Definitions, and sentences while you're offline, are translated on your device after the app downloads language files from Google.
  • Usage data for on-device tools. Text scanning and on-device translation use Google's ML Kit. Your images and text stay on your device, but ML Kit sends Google technical information, such as device details, app version, performance figures and the languages used, for diagnostics and usage analytics.
  • Voice input. Dictation uses your phone's speech service, so what you say is handled by that service's provider, usually Google.

Camera and photos

WordBank uses the camera, or a photo you choose, to read the text in an image. This happens on your device, and images aren't uploaded.

Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect information such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address, general location, device details, app performance and how you interact with the app and its ads. They use it to show and measure ads, for analytics, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalize ads.

Where the law requires it, WordBank asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether you own the purchase. Once you own it, the app stops showing ads.

Sharing and backup files

If you share a group or save a backup, WordBank creates a file and you decide where it goes. Once it leaves the app, it's up to you and whoever receives it.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account.
  • We don't receive your words, photos or practice history.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't add analytics of our own to the app.

Deleting your information

You can delete your words and groups in the app's Settings. Uninstalling WordBank removes everything stored in the app from your phone, though your own backup files and any Android backup remain until you delete them. Information collected by Google or the other services above is managed under their own policies.

Children

WordBank isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
